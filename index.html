Pinch with both hands to stretch a string. One hand alone does not draw.
Point and swipe to pluck. Two fingers play glass. A flat hand strums a chord.
Close a fist and strike down for the kick. Strike that fist sideways for the snare.
Hold a still fist to loop that bar. Drop the fist to stop the loop. Rest on a note and it walks up the scale.
Draw a circle with one finger to place a drum, then tap it. Wiggle a fist for a shaker.
Hold a pinch near a string for a drone, and raise that hand to play louder.
Pinch an end to retune. Drag the middle to move a string. Hold still on it to mute.
Hold a still open palm toward the camera to clear.
Tune (top right) sets the scale. Keys: D debug · Z undo · C clear · R record
